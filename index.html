About

I am a marine ecologist studying rocky-shore intertidal ecology, biodiversity, and climate change. My current work, as a Postdoctoral Research Fellow at the University of British Columbia (Harley Lab), asks what drives intertidal biodiversity across spatial scales, including solar irradiance, habitat complexity, and predation by keystone predators, in the Salish Sea of British Columbia.

Before UBC, I worked on echinoderm taxonomy, ecology, and fisheries in Atlantic Canada and the Arctic at Memorial University of Newfoundland, and on range expansions, intertidal invertebrates, and invasive mussels in southern Africa at Rhodes University.

I serve as an Associate Editor for Aquatic Invasions, BioInvasions Records, and Management of Biological Invasions, and as a Thematic Editor for the World Register of Introduced Marine Species (WRiMS). I co-supervise honours students and give outreach talks to public and stakeholder audiences.
